Radiant OS

A studio in your browser where you and a crew of dancing figures build small apps together, turn by turn, like a board game. You bring the ideas and you decide what ships. The crew does the building.

The floor is the tape of a two-dimensional Turing machine. Its heads — the Painter, the Dancer, the Critic and the Architect — paint it by one program, a council of them amends that program, and the veto is always yours. Everything it runs is a Jaren document.

The studio

  • Crew — start a project, add ideas, watch the crew build blocks, review and ship an app you can use. Up to four people take turns at one keyboard.
  • Board — notes and to-dos on a wall, linked into an order of work.
  • Mail — every conversation between people, the crew, the council and the docs.
  • Library — the documentation to read, to hear read aloud and to ask questions.
  • Settings — three art directions, sound and music, the camera, the light of the hour, motion and the language model's tool budget.

The arcade

  • Monkey Grove — a garden of number puzzles on a ladder of skills that grows with you.
  • Tape Dancers — write the rules a dancer follows on a Turing tape.
  • Bench Arena — benchmarks as a race, and the tool budget your language model can take.

Works offline once visited. Language models reach it over WebMCP. Source on GitHub.
